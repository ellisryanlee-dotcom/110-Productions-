# Episode 1 voice (scoped to this episode, not a general system)

Derived from sc-0000 (adopt list) + 110 brand. This is the voice the VO and
captions follow.

- **Stance:** calm expert, direct second person. We state trades, not miracles.
  Every "free" claims its cost in the same breath.
- **Sentences:** short declaratives. One idea each. No filler ("basically",
  "actually", "you know"), no hype inflation ("insane", "game-changer" banned).
- **Proof-first:** numbers appear on screen when spoken. Claims are demoable
  or they're cut.
- **Structure tics (from sc-0000):** agenda in the first 30 seconds; numbered
  steps spoken as numbers; one mid-video artifact drop; recap before outro.
- **Dry wit:** allowed twice per episode, never at the viewer's expense.
- **Analogy discipline:** one load-bearing analogy per episode, invented by
  us. Ep-001's is CONSOLE / CARTRIDGE (harness vs model). The source's
  analogy for this material is different and is not referenced.
- **Sign-off:** "Class dismissed."

Banned: emoji in VO/captions, "guys", rhetorical "right?", fake urgency.
