# First-Video Shortlist — 5 candidates, data-grounded

Picked where **demand is proven** (view counts in the niche) but **teacher
coverage is thin** (the gaps in `curriculum.md`). Each becomes a Brief
(`tools/writer.md`) once its cards exist. Titles use the house kernel —
outcome + proof + accessibility (see `voice-guide.md`).

## 1. The break-test video (evals/reliability) — RECOMMENDED FIRST
**Working title:** "I Tried to Break My AI Agent 50 Ways (so yours won't)"
**Why:** the single biggest gap — 57 of Nate's videos *touch* testing/reliability
(5.9M combined views prove the audience), but almost none make it the headline.
Our signature "break test" beat scales into a whole video. Draws on: Track 9
cards + agent-core cards. Artifact: a printable agent pre-launch checklist.

## 2. Credentials & security hygiene for agent stacks
**Working title:** "Your AI Agent Is One Leaked Key From Disaster (10-Minute Fix)"
**Why:** his one credentials tutorial pulled 257K views on a 4:43 video —
outsized demand per minute — and nobody in the lane owns security. Artifact:
credential-setup checklist + rotation schedule template.

## 3. Cost engineering across providers
**Working title:** "I Cut My AI Agent Bill 90% Without Making It Dumber"
**Why:** the "Ollama + Claude Code = 99% CHEAPER" video hit 530K — cost pain is
real, but coverage is newsy one-offs, not a system. We teach the decision
framework (when local models, when caching, when smaller tiers). Artifact:
cost-per-task calculator sheet.

## 4. Post-deployment: keeping client automations alive
**Working title:** "I Sold the Agent. Then the Real Work Started (maintenance
playbook)"
**Why:** the money content (8.2M views across 52 videos) stops at the sale —
"How I Sold These 4 AI Agents for $23,000" (463K) never covers month two.
Retainer/monitoring content serves the exact audience the teachers built.
Artifact: client maintenance-contract template.

## 5. The honest beginner path (positioning video)
**Working title:** "Stop Copying AI Gurus — Build These 3 Boring Agents That
Actually Get Used"
**Why:** contrarian entry video that states our angle (reliability > demos)
against a lane full of win-reels; beginner content is the lane's proven top of
funnel ("as a beginner" qualifier recurs across his top titles). Artifact: 3
starter templates.

---
**Sequencing logic:** #1 establishes the channel's signature and is the
strongest differentiator; #5 works as video two to plant the flag; #2–#4
follow as the library deepens. All five need Phase A/B (transcripts → cards)
completed first — briefs should cite real card ids, not vibes.
