# Hook (0:00–0:30)
Contrarian promise: states the topic (sell AI workflows) then immediately attacks the common advice (don't start an agency), positioning the video against the crowd. Adds personal-proof framing (how the creator went from zero to thousands per workflow without quitting a job) and previews the three-part payload: why not an agency, what to do instead, and how to sell step by step.

# Beat map
Essay/argument structure rather than a build. Roughly: hook and thesis → extended "why the agency path is a trap" section (personal cautionary story, role-multiplier reasoning) → "the alternative: consultant" → "first step: freelancer" → the how-to core (a four-step sell process) → "transitioning to consultant" (pricing/positioning shift) → big-picture recap → dual community CTA and outro.

# Retention devices
- Contrarian thesis creates curiosity gap (if not an agency, then what?).
- First-person confession/story beats (a mispriced early build) build trust and keep narrative momentum.
- Forward references ("remember those figures I mentioned") and a recap tie sections together.
- Concrete numbers and market-size claims spike perceived stakes mid-video.
- Named, sequential framework (freelancer → consultant → agency) gives a mental scaffold to hold attention.

# CTA placement
Deferred to the end: an offer of a free document via the free community, then a pitch for the paid community (with its weekly live Q&A and network as the value), closing on the standard like-and-outro. No interruptive CTA in the teaching body.
