# Hook (0:00–~1:30)
Pattern: milestone framing (an anniversary of a big life move) → promise of an "honest," both-sides take that cuts through polarized takes on the place → explicit two-part agenda (quick pros/cons, then who should and shouldn't do it). Personal-vlog hook built on authenticity and a promise of decision-useful verdicts rather than hype. No demo.

# Beat map
Personal reflection / opinion vlog:
- Context block: who the creator is and why he made the move (personal growth, geography, tax).
- Rapid-fire pros list (numbered, ~8 items), read briskly.
- Rapid-fire cons list (numbered, ~9 items), same cadence.
- Verdict section: profiles of who should move and who shouldn't (segmented list on each side).
- Reflective coda: a more personal, values-driven takeaway and the creator's own forward plan.

# Retention devices
- Numbered pro/con lists that promise a finite, skimmable structure.
- Personal, candid anecdotes and contrarian admissions to signal authenticity.
- Deferred payoff: the "who should you move / should you?" verdict is promised up front and held to the end.
- Emotional turn near the close (a more vulnerable reflection) to reward viewers who stay.

# CTA placement
- Minimal, non-commercial. A single soft pointer at the end to one of the creator's business-topic videos for viewers arriving from outside the core audience.
- No mid-roll product or community pitches; the video's genre is personal, so CTAs are light by design.
