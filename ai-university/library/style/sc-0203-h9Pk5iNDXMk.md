# Hook (0:00–~1:00)
Pattern: personal proof stacked fast (revenue figure + explicit disqualifiers removed — "no coding, no degree, no investors") → promise of the "exact blueprint" → social proof via community data (member counts and how many hit specific monthly-revenue tiers) → agenda preview of the three parts. The hook is credibility-by-numbers plus a promise of a step-by-step system.

# Beat map
Three-part structured guide:
- Part 1 — landscape: five beginner business models, each with a pros/cons pass and the creator's personal results, ending in a recommendation of one model.
- Part 2 — the three phases (learn → apply → monetize), each explained as a stage with concrete actions.
- Part 3 — a time-boxed roadmap (a multi-month plan) mapping the phases to a calendar, plus what free resources cover each step.
- Close: motivational stakes framing (economy changing, "winners vs. losers"), invitation to a live weekly community Q&A.

# Retention devices
- Community data points and member-earnings tiers as recurring proof.
- Consistent pros/cons rhythm across the five models so viewers can self-select.
- Personal anecdotes and specific dollar figures tied to each model.
- Forward-references to the roadmap ("we'll cover the exact steps next") that defer payoff.
- "Where should you start?" question posed, then answered, to resolve tension.

# CTA placement
- Recurring, low-friction CTA to a free community and a downloadable roadmap checklist ("first link in the description"), tied to the resources that fill each roadmap step.
