# Hook (0:00–~0:40)
Pattern: contrarian claim that upends the viewer's assumption about who wins (not the obvious specialists) → immediate credibility proof (a large personal revenue figure) → promise of the exact list the title advertises (why this is true, the specific skills, how to learn them from zero) → "this isn't just another guide, it's your blueprint." The hook is a contrarian thesis + proof + numbered promise.

# Beat map
Listicle-with-thesis, roughly:
- Brief self-intro/credibility.
- Theory section: an evolutionary-biology analogy argues that generalists out-survive specialists during rapid change; supported with statistics and a historical extinction example. (Analogy is load-bearing — describe abstractly for writers.)
- Reframe: defines the "AI generalist" and why the moment favors them (cites executive predictions).
- Core payload: four "powers," each unlocked by specific named skills/tools, delivered in order, each with a mini use-case.
- Synthesis: how the powers compound.
- Close: resource-guide CTA + callback to the opening biology quote.

# Retention devices
- Extended analogy that repeatedly promises to "bring it back around," creating an open loop.
- Statistics and famous-name predictions dropped as credibility anchors.
- Clear numbered structure (four powers / eight skills) so viewers know how far along they are.
- Concrete "old way vs. new way" cost/time contrasts for each skill.
- Personal proof sprinkled throughout (how the creator used each skill to earn).

# CTA placement
- Single consolidated CTA near the end: a free resource guide/roadmap gated behind joining a free community, framed as the shortcut to learning everything just covered.
- Light in-video mentions of an upcoming related video (subscribe teaser).
- Standard like/subscribe at the very end.
