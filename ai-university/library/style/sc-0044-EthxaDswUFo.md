undercut by "benchmarks are boring — I actually used these all day." Positions the
video as hands-on opinion over spec-sheet, promising a verdict by use case.

- 0:00 Release framing + benchmark tease + "I tested it for real" promise.
- ~1:30 Experiment 1: same prompt, two models, blind reveal (browser game build).
- ~5:00 Experiment 2: interactive website build, blind reveal + cost/token compare.

# Retention devices
Blind "guess which is which" reveals turn each test into a mini game (viewer forms
an opinion, then gets the answer). Running cost/token scoreboard creates a through-
line. Withholds the final verdict until the end (payoff loop).

community ask at the end; light and non-intrusive relative to the analysis.
