# Hook (0:00–~1:00)
Aspirational framing → credibility → promise-of-simplicity. Opens by tying the topic
to a bigger industry shift and a viewer benefit (make life easier / make money),
establishes the creator's track record and empathy for beginner intimidation, then
promises a from-absolute-zero walkthrough. No cold demo; the hook is a verbal contract
of "I'll make this easy."

- ~1:00–7:00 Concept teaching: traditional vs agentic automation; deterministic vs
  non-deterministic; when each fits. Two extended analogies used to make it concrete.

# Retention devices
- Two teaching analogies (navigation-with-a-map vs GPS; unorganized storage vs
  structured folders) reset attention before dense concept blocks.
- "Here's the cool part / best part" verbal signposts before payoffs.
- Open loops: promises to show self-fixing and the logo bug later, then delivers.
- Live artifact drops: folders appearing, files being generated, the PDF revealed —
  visible progress as pacing.
- Narrated "let me check in when it's done" jump-cuts compress long agent runs.
- Deliberately vague re-prompts framed as "let's see how good it does" to create
  suspense around agent capability.

follow-along file). Primary CTA at the very end: join the free community for all
resources, plus a standard like/subscribe ask. Affiliate/discount mention for a
third-party tool woven in naturally during the build (get-a-key step).
