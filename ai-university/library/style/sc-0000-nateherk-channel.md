# Channel Style Card — Nate Herk (metadata-derived)

## Output strategy
- **Cadence:** ~15 long-form videos/month (302 over 20 months) + ~8 shorts/month.
- **Length ladder:** median 17.4 min (p25 12.9 / p75 24.9). Ten 1hr+ tentpoles —
  free "(8+ Hour Course)" mega-courses — anchor authority and harvest search;
  everyday videos stay in the 13–25 min band.
- **Compounding format:** courses → free templates → community (skool) funnel.
  Nearly every build video ships a downloadable artifact.

## Title formulas (measured on 302 titles)
- "How to X / How I X" — 49 uses
- "I Built X" / "Build X" — 43 uses (result-first, first-person proof)
- Free/no-code packaging in title — 50 uses: "(free template)", "(No Code)",
  "(free n8n template)"
- Reassurance qualifier — "(as a beginner)" recurs on advanced topics
- Proof numbers — "$23,000", "500 AI Workflows", "95% in 36 Mins", "24/7"
- Trend-jacking names — "Karpathy Just 10x'd…", "Opus 4.8 Just Dropped"

**Kernel:** *outcome + first-person proof + accessibility disclaimer.*
"I Built [impressive thing] with [constraint: no code / $0 / 36 mins]
([free artifact])"

## Structure conventions (from 295 chapter lists)
- Chapter 1 is an agenda/promise ("Course Agenda", "What we're building")
- Numbered build steps as chapters ("1) RAG Pipeline & Chatbot…")
- A dedicated "free template" chapter mid-video (retention + CTA in one)
- News videos: claim → context → hands-on demo → "how to actually use it"

## Description conventions
- Line 1–2: community links (free course, paid community) before anything else
- Then: resource/template links, then timestamps
- CTA order never varies → the funnel is the business, videos are the top

## What we adopt vs. what stays reference
- Adopt: outcome-first titles with proof numbers; artifact-per-video; agenda-first
  chaptering; length ladder (17 min core / tentpole courses).
- Reference-only (do not clone): his catchphrases, thumbnails, persona, brand
  names ("skool" funnel specifics). Our channel defines its own voice guide on
  top of these structural patterns.
