# Hook (0:00–~1:30)
Cold-open montage of emotional swings. Rapid-fire clips of future moments (up money,
then "it crashed," then a recovery) tease volatility and outcome before any setup — a
highlight-reel spoiler designed to make you stay for the resolution. Then cuts to the
origin: two creators making a competitive wager with clear, simple rules and stakes.

# Beat map
- ~0:00–0:30 Emotional cold-open montage (spoiler-tease of the ups and downs).
- ~0:30–2:30 The challenge setup: rules, stakes, dual-creator format, agents that email
  each other for trash talk.
- ~2:30–5:00 Each builder explains their approach at a high level (engineered strategy
  vs. minimal "figure it out" prompt); show the accounts funded and first trades.
- ~5:00–15:00 Time-stamped check-ins (day 7, 15, 20, 22...) alternating between the two
  creators; portfolio numbers, strategy shifts, and comedic agent-to-agent emails as
  recurring bits. A mid-video giveaway mechanic is introduced.
- ~15:00–18:00 The reveal: staged in steps (day-7, day-20, then day-30 numbers) against
  a market-index baseline for drama; results discussed.
- ~18:00–18:56 Lessons learned, "what we'd do differently," what the agents themselves
  advise, and CTA.

# Retention devices
- Cold-open spoiler montage creates a "how did we get here?" loop.
- Two-protagonist rivalry format sustains tension the whole way.
- Recurring comedic device: the agents' sarcastic trash-talk emails as episodic beats.
- Time-stamped diary structure with a scoreboard-style number each check-in.
- Staged reveal (baseline first, then incremental days) delays the payoff for maximum
  tension.
- Mid-video giveaway tied to a comment action doubles as an engagement driver.

# CTA placement
Explicit repeated disclaimer (not financial advice) woven through, which also builds
trust. Mid-video giveaway CTA (comment to enter) placed at the natural lull before the
reveal. Cross-promotion of both creators' free communities near the end; standard like
ask in the outro. A "watch this related video" pointer during the lessons section.
