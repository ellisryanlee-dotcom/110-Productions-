# The freelancer to consultant to agency ladder

**What:** A staged path for making money with AI automations: start as a freelancer (paid to build and learn), graduate to consultant (paid for expertise and strategy), and only then consider building a team. Each stage earns the skills the next one requires.

**Why it matters:** Skipping stages means running before you can walk. The ladder protects your margins, builds real competence, and keeps the work enjoyable instead of leading to burnout.

**The moves:**
1. Freelancer: take builds to learn the end-to-end process and protect margins on your own dime.
2. Consultant: shift from "tell me what to build" to "let me diagnose what you need, then design it" — you're now paid for your brain, not your headcount.
3. Agency: only after you've mastered and can teach the whole process, build a team around proven skills, confidence, and repeatable processes.

**Watch out for:** The consultant stage is defined by being approached as the expert who leads the client, versus an agency owner mostly taking inbound build requests. Don't mistake more employees for more expertise.
