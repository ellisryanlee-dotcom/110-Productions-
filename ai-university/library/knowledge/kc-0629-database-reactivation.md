# Database reactivation: mine dormant contacts you already own

**What:** An automation that pulls forgotten contacts out of an existing CRM — churned
customers, subscribers who never bought, lapsed trials, leads who went quiet —
segments them by where they dropped off, and sends personalized outreach that
references each person's specific history to restart the conversation. Interested
replies get qualified and handed to sales as warm leads.

**Why it matters:** A business that's been around a while is usually sitting on a large
list of people who already know it and once showed interest, while spending all its
budget chasing new leads. Even a small reactivation rate can recover meaningful revenue
with no new ad spend — which makes it nearly self-selling once you show the owner the
size of their dormant list.

**The moves:**
1. Pull the existing contact database.
2. Segment by drop-off point / relationship stage.
3. Send personalized, history-aware outreach — no generic mass blasts.
4. Qualify responders and hand warm ones to a salesperson.

**Watch out for:** This is the opposite of fresh follow-up — it targets old contacts,
not new ones. It works best for businesses with high lifetime value / recurring
revenue and a database of several hundred-plus unworked contacts. The ROI figures
quoted are asserted; validate against the client's real list.

**Original example to invent:** Source used a local gym's member list. Writers should
use a different high-LTV business (e.g., a dental practice) and compute recovered
revenue with fresh assumptions.
