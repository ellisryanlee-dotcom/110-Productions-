# Close rate as a signal you're underpriced

**What:** Your proposal close rate tells you whether you're priced right. If you're
closing well above ~40–50% of proposals — clients rarely pushing back — that's the
market telling you you're too cheap. For B2B consulting, a ~20–30% close rate is
considered strong; little to no pricing friction is a warning sign, not a
celebration.

**Why it matters:** A sky-high close rate feels like success but usually means
clients see a steal and you're leaving margin (and customer lifetime value) on the
table. Treating close rate as a pricing gauge lets you raise rates with evidence
instead of nerve.

**The moves:**
1. Track what fraction of proposals you win.
2. If it's consistently above ~40–50%, raise your rates.
3. Aim for a healthy amount of friction — a ~20–30% close rate signals you're near
   the right level for B2B consulting.
4. Raise rates regularly rather than waiting for a crisis.

**Watch out for:** These thresholds are rules of thumb, not laws — calibrate to your
market and offer. Chronic zero-pushback is the tell that CLV is being left
uncaptured.
