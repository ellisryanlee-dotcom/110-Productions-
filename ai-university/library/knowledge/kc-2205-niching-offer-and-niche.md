# Niching down: one offer × one niche ("hotspot")

**What:** Rather than trying to serve the entire AI space, a solo provider should become an expert in one small, valuable intersection: a single offer (one system you can build well) sold to a single narrow niche of businesses. The goal is to own a tiny "hotspot" where your knowledge is far ahead of the buyer's.

**Why it matters:** Being a generalist AI shop requires a large expert team and is a brutal game; specializing lets a beginner get genuinely good fast, produce repeatable delivery, and market credibly to a specific audience. Repeatability also enables operational streamlining and higher win rates.

**The moves:**
1. Choose one system you'll master (the offer).
2. Choose a narrow niche of clueless, non-technical businesses.
3. Match offer to niche through testing (see the cold-email testing card) and go all-in on the pair that responds best.
4. Build repeatable processes and templates around that single pairing before expanding.

**Watch out for:** Premature diversification kills repeatability — "everything for everyone" leaves you with no reusable playbook. Don't confuse activity across many niches with progress; the point is to find and commit to one matchup.
