# The six-step skill-building framework

**What:** A repeatable checklist for designing a skill, covering everything from
what fires it to how it improves over time.

**Why it matters:** Having a fixed set of questions turns "write a skill" from a
blank page into a structured interview, and it's exactly what a skill-builder
helper can walk you through to gather the right information.

**The moves:**
1. **Name and trigger** — what it's called and the natural language that fires it.
2. **Goal** — in one sentence, the outcome/output the skill produces.
3. **Step-by-step process** — the exact manual procedure: what you do, in what
   order, what you look at, what decisions you make.
4. **Reference files** — the context it needs (images, current priorities, style
   guides, brand assets).
5. **Rules** — think about what could go wrong and add guardrails/constraints.
6. **Self-improvement loop** — plan to test, watch, and refine it repeatedly.

**Watch out for:** You will not write a perfect skill on the first try; the
framework front-loads the thinking but the sixth step (iteration) is where quality
actually comes from. Skipping the reference-files question is the common cause of
generic-feeling output.

**Original example to invent:** Source built an infographic skill live. Writers
should walk the six steps for a different skill.
