# The learn → apply → monetize path for a new agency

**What:** A three-phase sequence for going from zero to a paid AI service business. Phase 1 (learn): acquire the core skills — prompt engineering plus the three implementation areas of text chatbots, voice agents, and workflow automation — using free tutorials and no-code tools. Phase 2 (apply): take on the first one or two clients for free (often warm contacts) purely to gain real delivery experience and avoid beginner mistakes. Phase 3 (monetize): start charging, first with simple pricing, then niche down into a repeatable offer.

**Why it matters:** Beginners either over-study without shipping, or try to charge before they can deliver. Separating skill-building, unpaid real-world reps, and paid work removes both failure modes and builds confidence and proof before money is on the line.

**The moves:**
1. Spend the first block of time learning fundamentals hands-on, building sample chatbots / automations yourself.
2. Publicize availability in relevant communities and among friends/family; offer to solve a real problem for free.
3. Deliver those free projects end-to-end, treating client-adaptation and problem-solving as the real lesson.
4. Convert proof into paid work; publish wins to attract inbound.
5. Only after some paid delivery, niche down for repeatability (see the niching card).

**Watch out for:** The free-client phase is for learning, not martyrdom — cap it at a couple of clients. Skipping the "apply" phase and charging immediately tends to produce failed deliveries that damage reputation.
