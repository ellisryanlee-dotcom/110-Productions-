# Systemizing proof: testimonials and case studies

**What:** Turning past client results into reusable proof assets. Experience is worthless to prospects unless you can demonstrate it, so you deliberately collect testimonials and build simple one-page case studies (problem → solution → result, with hard numbers) that you then plug into all marketing and outreach.

**Why it matters:** Proof is the raw material for raising prices and lifting response rates. Outreach shifts from a cold pitch to a confident presentation of results you've already achieved, and social proof (especially video) shortens the trust gap.

**The moves:**
1. Ask your first clients for written testimonials; better yet, capture short video testimonials.
2. Write a one-page case study per client: the problem, what you did, and the measurable result.
3. Quantify wherever possible (e.g., percent reduction in response time, percent more inbound leads captured).
4. Publish these assets (site, professional network) and embed them in cold outreach.
5. Position yourself as the expert for the specific problem your niche has.

**Watch out for:** Metrics must be real and attributable, and client permission is required before publishing results. Vague or unverifiable claims undercut the proof.

**Original example to invent:** The source cites specific percentage outcomes. Writers should teach the case-study structure and invent their own numbers.
