# A catalog of sellable AI solution types

**What:** A demand-derived catalog of AI solutions businesses will pay for, grouped by delivery form. **Custom-GPT solutions** (cheap, chat-interface tools): documentation/SOP assistants, role-based work hubs, content-repurposing assistants, sales assistants. **AI agents** (deployed, tool-using): customer-support agents with action tools (refunds, order status), lead-generation website widgets, outbound SMS qualification agents, appointment-setters, and "AI persona" agents for creators/communities. **AI pipelines** (non-conversational bulk processing): lead-generation from bulk data, pre-qualification/call-note generation, email auto-responders that classify and reply, and CV/résumé evaluation for hiring. **Outbound voice-calling systems**: cold-call/first-touch, outbound support, surveys/follow-ups, and lead qualification/appointment-setting.

**Why it matters:** It's an idea bank for the "what do I sell?" problem, sorted by how the value is delivered, so a builder can match a solution form to a client's need and to their own skill level (GPT solutions are beginner-friendly; pipelines and voice calling are more advanced but higher-value).

**The moves:**
1. Match a client pain to the simplest delivery form that solves it (GPT vs agent vs pipeline vs voice).
2. Use support or another familiar function as a low-cost "foot in the door," then upsell higher-value items.
3. Favor forms that exploit high volume (see the volume-equals-value card).

**Watch out for:** Many of these ideas were early-stage when described; outbound voice calling in particular carries regulatory constraints (see the voice-regulation card). Reliability of multi-step agents is imperfect — keep humans in the loop for anything consequential.
