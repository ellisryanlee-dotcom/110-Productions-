# Two monetization paths: builder vs consultant (and the referral loop)

**What:** Two routes for turning AI skills into income, chosen by temperament. The **builder** enjoys hands-on technical work and gets clients by building a reputation in communities and on freelance platforms, delivering agents/automations for a few clients a month. The **consultant** is a people-person who prefers talking to owners; they run AI audits, strategy sessions, and team training rather than building. The two paths feed each other: a consultant who finds build work during an audit can hand it to a builder and take a cut.

**Why it matters:** Matching the path to who you are increases follow-through. The referral loop between the two roles means neither has to do the part they dislike — builders get deals, consultants get delivery, both get paid.

**The moves:**
- **Builder:** apply the skills to real projects, gather experience, build reputation in communities and on freelance marketplaces, raise rates as you improve.
- **Consultant:** learn enough of the technical basics to understand what's possible, then sell AI audits (progressing from free to paid) and workshops; pass surfaced build work to builders for a cut.
- Both: pick one path, then pursue a concrete first paid client as the initial milestone.

**Watch out for:** Even the consultant needs baseline technical literacy to scope credibly. Referral arrangements need clear terms so the cut and responsibilities are unambiguous.
