# Why starting an agency first is a trap

**What:** The popular advice to launch an AI-automation agency as your first move is usually wrong. Before you've personally run the full journey — outreach, scoping, pricing, building, delivering, supporting — you can't lead a team through it, and the agency structure multiplies every mistake you haven't yet learned to avoid.

**Why it matters:** Mispricing and under-scoping are near-guaranteed when you're new; inside an agency those errors compound across developers, salespeople, and multiple clients, hurting margins, brand, and reputation instead of just one project.

**The moves:**
1. Recognize that as a beginner you'll under-scope and misprice; that's tuition you pay solo, cheaply.
2. Understand the multiplier: a bad price or proposal handed to a developer or setter becomes a bad delivery at scale.
3. See the real killer — juggling many clients with different expectations and timelines simultaneously.
4. Learn the whole delivery cycle yourself first so you can eventually direct others through it.

**Watch out for:** Closing lots of sales before you can reliably deliver creates a "stress machine," not a business — over-selling capacity you can't fulfill damages your brand.
