# Niching down into a repeatable productized service

**What:** After gaining broad experience, focus the business on solving one specific problem for one specific type of business. Instead of custom-building every engagement from scratch, you master a single solution and lightly adapt it to each new client in the same niche — turning bespoke project work into a near-productized, repeatable service.

**Why it matters:** Repeatability is where agency profit and scalability come from. A narrow niche means faster delivery, higher margins (you reuse most of the build), easier marketing (you become the go-to specialist), and predictable growth, versus reinventing the solution for every unrelated client.

**The moves:**
1. From your delivered projects, identify one problem + one customer type where demand is real and results are strong.
2. Standardize that solution into a template you can redeploy.
3. For each new client, adapt the template to their specifics rather than rebuilding.
4. Market yourself as the specialist for that problem/niche to attract inbound.
5. Reinvest the time saved into sales and further standardization.

**Watch out for:** Niching too early (before you've validated a repeatable win) can lock you into a weak market. Niche selection should be driven by evidence from real deliveries, not a guess.
