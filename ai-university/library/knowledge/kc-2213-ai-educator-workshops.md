# The AI educator model: workshops and team training

**What:** A non-technical way to make money from AI by teaching businesses how to use it — running live workshops for companies or creating courses for their teams on current tools. You don't need to be an expert; you only need to know meaningfully more than the audience, which for most non-technical companies is a low bar.

**Why it matters:** Training an existing workforce to be more productive is easier for companies than hiring, so there's strong demand. Education is also often the *first* step a business takes into AI before it's ready to buy build work — positioning you for later services.

**The moves:**
1. Find high-quality explainer content on a topic and use an LLM to turn a transcript into a workshop or course plan.
2. Generate slides quickly with an AI slide tool.
3. Run the first session free via warm/second-degree connections ("do you know anyone who'd want this?").
4. Refine, then charge for subsequent sessions.
5. Add a memorable live demo (e.g., a voice agent) to create a "wow" moment regardless of audience.

**Watch out for:** You must actually be a step ahead and able to field room questions; repackaging content isn't enough if you can't handle live Q&A. Pricing power grows with reputation, not on day one.
