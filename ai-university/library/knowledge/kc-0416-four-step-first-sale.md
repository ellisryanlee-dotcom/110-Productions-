# A four-step process to land your first AI-workflow deals

**What:** A concrete sequence for going from zero to a paid workflow: pick a painpoint, prove it with a quick demo, make an irresistible offer, then deliver while capturing before/after metrics.

**Why it matters:** "I can automate anything" is too broad to convert. Niching and a repeatable sales motion raise close rates, let you charge more, and produce the case studies that win the next client.

**The moves:**
1. Choose a specific audience and one clear painpoint to solve for them rather than offering everything to everyone.
2. Build a short demo or case study showing a concrete before/after of the result.
3. Reach out with an offer framed in the prospect's language of time and money so declining feels foolish.
4. Deliver, then measure the same metrics before and after (task time, error rate, volume handled) to prove value objectively and earn a strong testimonial.

**Watch out for:** Specialization commands premium pricing; staying a generalist competes on price. Skipping metric capture forfeits your best future-sales asset.

**Original example to invent:** Source mentions a coach's chatbot demo. Writers should design a different niche/painpoint pair and the specific before/after metrics they'd capture.
