# Increasing revenue per client: upsells, AI audits, and retainers

**What:** A set of moves to multiply revenue from an existing client rather than only chasing new ones. A first deliverable (e.g., a chatbot) acts as a low-risk entry point that earns trust; from there you upsell adjacent automations, then graduate to full business audits/workshops and ongoing retainers.

**Why it matters:** Landing a client is the hard part; expanding an existing relationship is far cheaper and raises lifetime value. Once you can offer many deliverables, moving a client from per-project to a monthly partnership is an easy sell and creates recurring income.

**The moves:**
1. Use the first, easy deliverable as a trust-building entry point.
2. Upsell immediate adjacencies (integrations, analytics on the data the first system produces, internal-facing tools).
3. Offer an AI audit: analyze the whole operation, identify where AI can save time or make money, and get paid both for the audit and for building what it surfaces.
4. Package education (training their team), consulting (ongoing strategy), and development (building systems) into a transformation offer.
5. Convert to a retainer once you have a menu of proven deliverables — pitch it as saving them scoping time and money versus quoting each item.

**Watch out for:** Upselling only works after you've actually delivered value — lead with proof, not a pitch. Full audits/transformation offers require broader business understanding than a single build.
