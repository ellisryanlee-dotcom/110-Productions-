# Pricing with the value equation and a setup-fee-plus-retainer model

**What:** A simple pricing heuristic: the price you can charge scales with your skill multiplied by your experience. A beginner with modest skill and no track record charges little; after delivering for several clients you can prove results, so you can raise prices and change the pricing *shape*. The recommended shape as you mature is an upfront setup fee plus a monthly retainer, rather than a bare retainer.

**Why it matters:** It gives a rationale for raising prices (you're now selling a proven result, not a promise) and a cash-flow lever. An upfront fee improves cash flow immediately and filters out low-commitment "tire-kicker" prospects from serious clients.

**The moves:**
1. Start low to win your first clients and gather results (a discounted "case-study rate" is acceptable early).
2. Convert each delivery into demonstrated outcomes you can cite.
3. As experience grows, raise prices and introduce an upfront setup fee alongside the monthly retainer.
4. Bundle multiple deliverables into the retainer once you have a menu of proven services.

**Watch out for:** Raising price without proof erodes trust — the experience half of the equation is what justifies it. Heavy discounting should be deliberate (e.g., to earn a case study), not a default.
