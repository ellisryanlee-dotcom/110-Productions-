# Qualify clients by their mindset, not just their budget

**What:** A willing-to-pay client isn't automatically a good client. The best partners see AI as a forward-looking investment; the worst are desperate or deeply skeptical and expect AI to rescue a failing business.

**Why it matters:** A poorly matched client makes the project miserable regardless of fee, and pressure from someone treating AI as a last-ditch lifeline sets both sides up to fail.

**The moves:**
1. In discovery, ask what they think of AI and where they think it's going.
2. Read the answer: enthusiasm and a bullish outlook signal a good partner; hesitation or skepticism signals resistance ahead.
3. Steer away from clients who are risk-averse or hoping AI plugs every hole in a sinking ship.
4. Prioritize forward-thinking owners who want to get ahead of the curve.

**Watch out for:** Even a large check can be a bad deal if the client's expectations of AI are magical or their business is in crisis.
