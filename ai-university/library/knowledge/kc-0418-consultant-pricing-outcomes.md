# Consultant pricing: charge for systems and outcomes, not tasks

**What:** Once you have proof, you reposition from task-builder to system-designer. Instead of quoting a single automation, you package a full system tied to a business outcome, which supports much higher prices, retainers, and advisory relationships.

**Why it matters:** A one-off build is worth a fraction of an integrated system that runs continuously and keeps generating value. Framing the same underlying work as a business-changing system multiplies what clients will pay.

**The moves:**
1. Stop quoting isolated tasks; quote a designed system tied to a measurable outcome delivered over a defined period.
2. Sell strategy as a deliverable — a document mapping where AI should and shouldn't be used can be worth more than the workflows themselves.
3. Pursue longer-term engagements: monthly retainers, ongoing advisory, or revenue-share partnerships.
4. Position against the market gap: large firms book enormous AI-consulting revenue, but small and mid-size businesses can't afford them and still need guidance — you serve that segment at a fraction of the price.

**Watch out for:** Roughly similar work commands very different fees depending on framing; underselling it as "just a chatbot" leaves most of the value on the table.

**Original example to invent:** Source contrasts a fixed chatbot quote with a framed lead-qualification system. Writers should invent a different task-vs-system reframe with fresh numbers and cite the market-gap idea generically.
