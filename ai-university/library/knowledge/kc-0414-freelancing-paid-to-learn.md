# Use freelancing to get paid to learn

**What:** The freelancing stage exists to bank three to five solid paid results while you optimize for experience over income. You're building the proof and the playbook that make consulting possible.

**Why it matters:** Without demonstrated results, testimonials, process confidence, and niche clarity, moving up to higher-ticket consulting is guesswork done with a demanding client watching.

**The moves:**
1. Target three to five real, paid wins you can show as evidence.
2. Optimize each project for skill and speed, not maximum fee — treat it as paid practice.
3. Harvest four assets from every job: proof of results, a testimonial, confidence in running a project start to finish, and clarity on which clients you like and who pays well.
4. If a first project or two for free (or cheap) buys a strong case study and testimonial, it can be worth it.

**Watch out for:** Chasing max revenue on early deals sacrifices the learning that makes later deals big; the goal is a repeatable playbook, not one good invoice.
