# Productize AI-built websites for local businesses

**What:** A business model built on the fast website workflow: many businesses
have poor sites but won't pay a traditional agency tens of thousands of dollars or
wait months. You can pick a niche, build a strong demo site in about a day, then
approach prospects with a working link and offer a custom version fast and far
cheaper than incumbents — plus recurring revenue from hosting and maintenance.

**Why it matters:** The speed of AI builds turns website work into a repeatable,
niche-able offer with both project fees and recurring income, and the maintenance
tie-in is defensible because you understand and can quickly change the codebase.

**The moves:**
1. Choose a niche and build a polished demo site for it as your pitch asset.
2. Prospect within that niche; lead with the live demo link.
3. Offer a fast turnaround at a fraction of incumbent pricing.
4. Add recurring hosting/maintenance so feature changes and fixes are an ongoing
   service, not a one-off.
5. Customize your build skill to your house style so output matches your brand.

**Watch out for:** A quick demo isn't a finished product — budget more time for a
real client site (copy, mobile, polish). Recurring maintenance is a commitment,
not just an upsell.

**Original example to invent:** Pick a specific local niche and outline the demo
site, the pitch, and the project + recurring pricing you'd propose.
