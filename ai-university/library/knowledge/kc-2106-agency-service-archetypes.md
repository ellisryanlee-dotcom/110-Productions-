# What to sell: AI agency service archetypes and specializations

**What:** Two complementary menus for a beginner deciding what to offer — concrete deliverables to sell, and solution-type specializations to build expertise in.

**Why it matters:** Beginners stall on "what do I actually sell?" These give proven, buildable offers that are easy to understand yet high-value, and a way to niche your skill set so you can become an "expert" in one to two months.

**The moves — beginner deliverables (pick offers that are easy to build AND high-value):**
1. Personalized cold-outreach system: pull data on a lead, inject into a prompt, generate a tailored message, send — lifts response rates on leads the client already pays for.
2. Staff-training / internal-knowledge chatbot: ingest training docs and FAQs into a knowledge base; cuts onboarding hours.
3. Document/plan generator: store a client's past documents in a vector store, retrieve similar ones, and generate new plans or contracts — big time-saver in plan-heavy niches.
4. Onboarding chat assistant: map an onboarding flow into a bot that captures info, generates contracts, and connects tools.
5. Customer/guest support chatbot: ingest a business's FAQ; deflect the bulk of repetitive queries; benefits both the business and its end-customers.

**The moves — solution-type specializations (become the go-to for one):**
- Workflow-automation specialist (visual automation platform + a database tool).
- Text-based agent specialist (assistant APIs / hosted GPT-style assistants).
- Voice-agent specialist (voice-agent platforms).
- Single-ecosystem specialist (go deep on one automation ecosystem's full stack).
- Chatbot-flow specialist (node-based bot builders with structured flows).

**Watch out for:** Go narrow first — one niche, one deliverable — then broaden. Wide-but-shallow offers are hard to market and reuse.
