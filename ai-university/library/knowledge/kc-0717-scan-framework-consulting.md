# SCAN: running AI consulting engagements

**What:** A four-step framework for the consulting stage, where you sell clarity
(what to automate) rather than a single automation. SCAN = Study the business
(discovery calls about bottlenecks, recurring tasks, staffing costs, complaints),
Calculate the opportunity (translate inefficiency into dollars, e.g., a process
costing $200k/yr that AI cuts 70% = $140k saved), Architect the solution (a
roadmap mixing plug-in templates and custom builds across departments), Narrate
the results (turn ROI case studies into content on your site and social to attract
more clients).

**Why it matters:** Most businesses know AI matters but not where to start, so the
diagnosis is the product. Framing savings in black-and-white turns your fee from a
cost into an investment with a measurable return, which supports charging for the
audit and again for implementation.

**The moves:**
1. Run discovery calls to surface real bottlenecks and their current cost.
2. Convert those costs into a concrete savings number the owner can see.
3. Design a roadmap; use templates for quick wins and custom builds where needed.
4. Publish the resulting ROI case studies as content so clients come to you.

**Watch out for:** This stage needs both technical range and consulting
credibility, so it's harder for beginners. The client's stated ask may not be
the highest-leverage opportunity — discovery often reveals a different, bigger
one. Price on value so the ROI makes the fee a no-brainer.

**Original example to invent:** Invent a business, run the SCAN steps, and produce
the one-line savings figure you'd put in front of the owner.
