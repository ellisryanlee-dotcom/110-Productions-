# The AI Automation Agency (AAA) business model

**What:** A one-person-or-small-team service business that helps other businesses adopt AI. Instead of writing custom software, you deliver working solutions (chatbots, automations, agents, content systems) using low-code/no-code tools. You can charge per deliverable (a project fee) or bundle several deliverables into a monthly retainer for recurring revenue. Your real product is your ability to apply AI to a business and get a measurable result — not code.

**Why it matters:** It's a low-startup-cost, low-risk on-ramp for non-developers to build income around AI. Because you get paid before delivering (or can offer refunds while freelancing), risk is minimal, and the no-code tooling means you can hire and train contractors from a large talent pool rather than needing elite engineers.

**The moves:**
1. Position yourself as the intermediary that helps a business reduce expenses and/or increase revenue with AI.
2. Choose whether you serve one deliverable at a time (project pricing) or package multiple into a retainer.
3. Use low-code/no-code builders so delivery doesn't require custom engineering.
4. Frame the outcome, not the technology, when selling — the buyer wants a result and peace of mind.
5. Reinvest early client results into proof (see the case-studies card) to command higher prices over time.

**Watch out for:** Higher-ticket custom-development work carries heavy admin (calls, scoping, communication) and doesn't scale for a beginner; the lower-ticket no-code niche is often more scalable and predictable. The model rewards patience — it compounds as your track record grows, the inverse of businesses that are easy to start but decay over time.
