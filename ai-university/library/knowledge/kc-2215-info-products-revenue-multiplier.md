# Info products as a revenue multiplier

**What:** Packaging the expertise you've proven in your service business into a course or training program for your niche, so you can sell knowledge repeatedly without re-delivering it each time. Presented as a late-stage move once you have real results to teach.

**Why it matters:** Services are capped by how many clients you can deliver for; an info product is capped only by how many buyers you can reach. Combining a service business with an info business is framed as a path to substantially higher, more scalable income.

**The moves:**
1. Prove a repeatable result in your agency first — the product's credibility comes from that.
2. Distill the playbook into a niche-specific course (e.g., an operations playbook for a specific trade).
3. Attach it to your content/personal-brand distribution so you have an audience to sell to.
4. Run it alongside services rather than replacing them (services build the proof; the product scales it).

**Watch out for:** An info product without genuine underlying expertise and results is hollow — sequence it after the service success, not before. It also needs an audience/distribution to sell into, which ties back to content.

**Original example to invent:** The source cites a specific creator's monthly info-product revenue as proof. Writers should make the services-plus-products point with a hypothetical example.
