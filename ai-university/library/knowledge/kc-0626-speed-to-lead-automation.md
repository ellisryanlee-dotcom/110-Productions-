# Speed-to-lead: respond to new inquiries within seconds

**What:** An automation that fires the instant someone submits a form, inquiry, or
call. It captures the lead's info, qualifies them against criteria (budget, location,
service type), routes them to the right team member with context, and sends a
personalized follow-up — all within seconds instead of hours or days.

**Why it matters:** Response speed strongly predicts conversion; the presenter cites
that replying within a few minutes can multiply conversion versus even a 30-minute
delay, while the average business takes many hours to respond. Closing that gap raises
close rate without changing ad spend, copy, or offer — which makes the ROI easy to
show and easy to sell to any service business.

**The moves:**
1. Trigger on form submit / inquiry / call.
2. Capture and structure the lead's details.
3. Qualify against defined criteria and route to the right person with full context.
4. Immediately send a personalized confirmation/follow-up across channels.

**Watch out for:** These conversion statistics are asserted, not demonstrated on
screen — use them directionally, not as guarantees. The win comes from making the
existing business faster; it won't fix a weak offer.

**Original example to invent:** Source walked a dental-clinic close-rate scenario.
Writers must build the value math for a different service business (e.g., a roofing
company) with fresh numbers.
