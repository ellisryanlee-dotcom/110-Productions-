# SHARE: the teaching and content funnel

**What:** A five-step framework for the teaching stage — the highest-margin model,
where you monetize expertise through content and community. SHARE = Show
(demonstrate genuinely unique, impressive automations), Hook (offer a free
resource to build trust and start a community), Attract (warm the free group up with
a steady drip of value), Recommend (present a paid community as the
natural next step for deeper access), Expand (keep producing content and templates
that grow audience and recurring revenue). The funnel is: videos → free community
→ paid community.

**Why it matters:** Teaching can be almost pure profit (no payroll or big
expenses) and scales infinitely via subscriptions, courses, and sponsorships. It
also compounds the earlier stages, since sharing case studies already builds the
audience and trust this stage monetizes.

**The moves:**
1. Publish content showing automations that make viewers say "I didn't know you
   could do that."
2. Give away a genuinely useful free resource to convert viewers into a community.
3. Consistently deliver value in the free space to build trust.
4. Offer a paid tier for deeper support, advanced templates, and live access.
5. Keep shipping content and templates so audience and recurring revenue grow.

**Watch out for:** This is the hardest stage — there's no hiding weak skills, so
your automations must be sharp and battle-tested. You don't need to be the world
expert, but you must know more than the people you're teaching. Audience-building
is slow but converts once established; consistency is the requirement.

**Original example to invent:** Design a teaching funnel for a specific niche —
one flagship free resource, the free-community value cadence, and the paid-tier
offer.
