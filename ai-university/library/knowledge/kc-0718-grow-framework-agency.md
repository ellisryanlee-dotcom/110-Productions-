# GROW: scaling into an AI partner/agency business

**What:** A four-step framework for turning solo consulting into a team business
(an "AI partner" focused on long-term relationships rather than one-off projects).
GROW = Get developers (hire builders to offload technical delivery so you stay
client-facing), Retain authority (keep yourself as the strategist running audits
and system design), Onboard sales (bring in reps/appointment setters once delivery
is stable to keep the pipeline full), Win with brand (scale your personal brand
and case studies so you're a trusted name in your niche).

**Why it matters:** You eventually hit a ceiling on how many calls, designs, and
builds you can do alone. Building a team multiplies your time and lets you close
bigger deals, targeting the large mid-market that can't afford big consulting
firms but needs the same diagnosis + implementation.

**The moves:**
1. Offload the most time-consuming technical builds to developers first.
2. Stay positioned as the strategist and relationship owner.
3. Add sales support only after delivery is reliable.
4. Invest in brand and case studies as your top-of-funnel.

**Watch out for:** Only start here after mastering consulting — a team adds many
moving pieces. Expenses (salaries, overhead, ads) can consume half of a
million-dollar agency's revenue, so revenue is not profit. Readiness checklist:
steady clients you can close, ROI case studies, more work than you can handle,
and a brand strong enough to attract inbound.

**Original example to invent:** Draft the first three hires for a fictional agency
and the trigger (workload/metric) that would justify each.
