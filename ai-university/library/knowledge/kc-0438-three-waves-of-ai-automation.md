# The three waves of AI automation

**What:** A framing of how AI automation has evolved. Wave one was conversational chatbots — impressive, but mostly talking, not doing work. Wave two combined that AI brain with automation platforms to take real action: classifying tickets, personalizing emails, summarizing documents, building full tool-using agents. Wave three is agentic building tools that change how you build — natural language becomes the interface and the agent handles implementation.

**Why it matters:** Each wave didn't kill the last; it was a stepping stone that made builders more productive. Understanding the progression clarifies why the current shift is continuous, not a rupture, and why earlier skills still count.

**The moves:**
1. Recognize wave one: conversational AI that generated content but mostly didn't act.
2. Recognize wave two: AI plugged into automation platforms and APIs to take multi-step action with memory and tools.
3. Recognize wave three: agentic tools that keep what you build but transform how — describe outcomes, the agent implements.
4. See that each wave layered on the previous rather than replacing it.

**Watch out for:** Treating a new wave as making prior approaches obsolete is a mistake — earlier methods still deliver value and form the foundation for the next.

**Original example to invent:** Source anchors waves to specific product launches. Writers should illustrate the progression with a different through-line without reusing those specific milestones as claims.
