# Finding your first clients by cold-email niche/offer testing

**What:** A structured way to discover which offer resonates with which niche using cold outreach as a market test. You pick a handful of candidate niches, send a simple campaign for one offer to each, and read the response rates to find the niche/offer pairing with the strongest interest — then concentrate there.

**Why it matters:** It removes guesswork from positioning. Instead of committing to a niche on a hunch, you let real reply rates tell you where demand is, and you only need a small number of clients (e.g., a few paying a monthly fee) to hit a meaningful income target.

**The moves:**
1. Select several non-technical niches to test.
2. Choose one offer and send a simple cold-email campaign to each niche.
3. Compare interest by niche; if an offer flops across niches, swap to the next offer and retest.
4. Identify the pairing that replies at a clearly higher rate and go all-in on it.
5. As you gain proof, fold testimonials and case studies into the email copy to lift response (see the proof card).

**Watch out for:** Cold email has deliverability, volume, and compliance considerations; scripts need iteration and split-testing. Response rate measures interest, not closed revenue — still qualify and sell after the reply.
