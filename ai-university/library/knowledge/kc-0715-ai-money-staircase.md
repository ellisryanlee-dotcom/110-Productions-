# The AI-money staircase: freelancer → consultant → agency → teacher

**What:** A four-stage progression for building income with AI automation, meant
to be climbed in order because each stage produces the proof and skills the next
one needs. Stage 1 freelancer: use pre-built templates to land small projects and
build case studies. Stage 2 consultant: audit businesses, quantify ROI, design
roadmaps, and charge for both the audit and the implementation. Stage 3 agency
(or "partner"): hire developers and sales so you scale beyond your own hours.
Stage 4 teacher: package your knowledge into content and communities for
high-margin recurring revenue. Each stage carries a scorecard: speed to money,
ease for beginners, and income potential.

**Why it matters:** It reframes "make money with AI" as stacking skills in
sequence rather than picking one model. Freelancing gets proof, consulting gets
credibility, agency gets scale, teaching gets freedom — and skipping ahead (e.g.,
starting at agency) is "running before you walk."

**The moves:**
1. Start at freelancing to generate proof and confidence.
2. Let the case studies from freelancing open the door to consulting.
3. Scale into an agency only once you have steady clients, ROI case studies, more
   work than you can handle, and a brand.
4. Move to teaching last, when your automations and results can withstand public
   scrutiny.

**Watch out for:** The stages have opposite scorecard profiles — early stages pay
faster but cap lower; later stages are harder to reach but scale higher. Don't
skip stages; the earlier ones create the assets the later ones require.
