# Follow-up and nurture sequences for fresh warm leads

**What:** An automation that, on a trigger event (form fill, webinar attendance, a
booked call), kicks off a personalized multi-touch sequence automatically. Different
paths handle different states (e.g., a no-show gets the replay), each touch adds real
value, and the moment the lead replies or books, the sequence stops and a salesperson
is notified with full context. Over time you can layer in CRM data and live research
to personalize further.

**Why it matters:** Most sales need several follow-ups, but people typically stop after
one or two, letting warm leads — who already raised their hand — go cold. Converting a
warm lead is far cheaper than acquiring a cold one, so consistent automated follow-up
recovers revenue the business already paid to generate.

**The moves:**
1. Trigger on the intent event.
2. Run a personalized sequence of several value-adding touches over days/weeks.
3. Branch by state (attended vs. no-show, replied vs. silent).
4. Stop instantly on reply/booking and hand off to sales with context.
5. Enrich with CRM data or research to keep each touch personal.

**Watch out for:** This is distinct from reactivating old contacts — it's for leads who
just came in and need more touchpoints. It only helps businesses that already have
decent lead volume; if getting leads is the bottleneck, fix that first. The cited
conversion lifts are illustrative, not demonstrated.

**Original example to invent:** Source used a B2B webinar funnel. Writers should build
a different nurture scenario (e.g., a trial-signup sequence) with their own numbers.
