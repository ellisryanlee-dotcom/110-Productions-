## Voice (defaults until overridden)

- Plain, everyday language; zero gatekeeping; explain acronyms on first use.
- First-person builder proof ("I built, I tested, I sold") — never "one might".
- Honest about failures and costs; we show the error message, the invoice, the
  retry. This is our differentiator: teachers in this lane mostly show wins.
- No hype-words: "insane", "game-changer", "you won't believe" are banned.
- Humor: dry, brief, never at the audience's expense.

## Packaging formulas (measured, adopt)

- **Title kernel:** outcome + first-person proof + accessibility qualifier.
  Pattern: "I Built [impressive thing] with [constraint] ([free artifact])".
- Proof numbers in titles when true ("$X", "N workflows", "in NN minutes").
- **Length ladder:** core videos 13–25 min (aim 17); occasional 1h+ tentpole
  course; shorts cut from long-form later.
- **Every build video ships an artifact** (template, checklist, repo) —
  mentioned mid-video, linked in description.
- **Chapters:** first chapter = agenda/promise; numbered build steps; a
  dedicated artifact chapter mid-video.
- **Description order:** artifact link → resources → timestamps. [ELLIS —
  community/funnel link once one exists]

## Structure defaults per video

1. Hook (≤30s): show the finished result working, then the promise + agenda.
2. Context (≤90s): why this matters / what it costs to not have it.
3. The build in numbered steps, each step = chapter.
4. The break test: try to make it fail on camera, fix it. (Our signature —
   reliability is the gap every teacher leaves open.)
5. Artifact drop + what to build next (CTA).

## Hard rules (from tools/librarian.md — non-negotiable)

- Scripts are written from knowledge cards only; writers never see transcripts.
- No copied phrasing, analogies, examples, or thumbnails from any teacher.
- Every factual claim traces to a card or a verified doc; steps must be tested.
- Realistic AI-generated visuals/voiceover get YouTube's synthetic-media label.
